# ❄️ Owen's Frozen Games

A gentle, kid-friendly browser game made for a 4-year-old — a *Frozen*-themed mystery you can play with just the **arrow keys** on a computer, or with **on-screen buttons** on a phone or tablet. No install, no internet needed, no way to "lose."

**▶️ Play it here:** https://ian-sacramentolabs.github.io/owens-frozen-games/

*(Or download `index.html` and double-click it — it runs fully offline in any browser.)*

---

## 🎮 How to play

**On a computer — arrow keys:**

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **← →** | Walk |
| **↑** | Jump |
| **🏠 button** (top-right) | Back to the menu |
| **🔊 button** (top-right) | Sound on / off |

**On a phone or tablet — touch buttons:** big **◀ ▶** buttons (bottom-left) to walk and a large **⬆** button (bottom-right) to jump appear automatically on touch screens. The 🏠 and 🔊 buttons work with a tap too.

From the menu, use **← →** (or the touch buttons) to choose a story and **↑** (or ⬆) to start — or just **tap a card**. Then **pick who you want to be** (Anna, Elsa, Kristoff, Sven, or *Everyone!* for the classic mix).

**Your name:** the first time you open the game it asks *"Who's playing?"* — a grown-up types the child's name and it appears throughout the game ("Sofia's Frozen Games", "Great job, Sofia!"). The name is remembered on that device, and the **✏️ button** (top-right) changes it anytime.

**Settings (⚙️ button):** toggles for **🗣️ Talking** (spoken narration), **🎵 Music**, **🌙 Calmer motion** (less snow/sparkle for sensitive kids — on automatically if the device prefers reduced motion), **🧸 Bigger hero**, and **🐢 Slower moving**. Choices are remembered on the device.

## 🕵️ The three stories

- **🥕 Sven's Lost Carrots** — Sven's carrot snacks went missing all over **Elsa's ice castle**! Explore four grand rooms (Great Ice Hall, Frozen Grand Stair, Crystal Ballroom, and the Snowflake Throne Room) as **Anna, Elsa, Kristoff, and Sven**, jumping onto platforms to collect all six carrots.
- **⛄ Olaf's Missing Pieces** — Gale the wind spirit blew Olaf apart! Find his nose, arms, buttons, and hair. Watch the little Olaf in the corner rebuild himself piece by piece.
- **⛄ Little Snowgies** — Olaf's tiny snowgie friends are hiding across four snowy places. Find all six and watch them giggle!

## 👶 Made for little kids

- **Arrow keys** on a computer, or **big touch buttons** on a tablet/phone. You can also **tap cards** directly.
- **Personalized** — enter the child's name once and it's woven through the whole game.
- **Reads aloud & counts out loud** — spoken encouragement, and each piece is counted ("one!… two!…") with a big number, for gentle early math.
- **Choose your hero** and an **accessibility menu** (calmer motion, bigger hero, slower moving).
- **Gentle background music** and a friendly **snow-bunny** to say hello to in every level.
- **Room dots** show progress, and levels **advance on their own** once cleared — no one gets stuck.
- **No fail state, no timer, no enemies** — impossible to get stuck or lose.
- Big, colorful pictures with minimal reading.
- Gentle built-in sound effects (mutable).

## 🛠️ Tech

A single self-contained `index.html` file — HTML5 Canvas, plain JavaScript, the Web Audio API (sound & music), the Web Speech API (narration), and `localStorage` (name & settings). No frameworks, no external assets, no build step.
